For TODO 4 (cg_ticker null Float64 fields):
  - The pre-existing _wconvert(::Type{Union{Nothing, T}}, v) helper at utils.jl:21
    already handles Union{Nothing,T} fields by short-circuiting on nothing
    and recursing to T otherwise.
  - The bug was that the @parsedata macro called fromdict WITHOUT the
    _wkey/_wconvert kwargs (using defaults kconvfunc=convert, convfunc=convert).
    For fromdict defaults with String-keyed dicts, `convert(String, :fid)`
    fails (no such Julia method), and even when keys work, the convert-based
    path doesn't short-circuit `convert(Float64, nothing)`.
  - The fix: macro now passes _wkey and _wconvert. _wkey(::Type{String}, k)
    = string(k) handles Symbol→String. _wconvert correctly maps
    Union{Nothing,Float64}+nothing → nothing.
